Flat Manager Guide

Most important information is on the dashboard, but this page explains how to record transactions properly.

Rent and $25 expenses

Stella will record all rent and $25 expenses payments once received. You do not need to enter these yourself.

Recording a Transaction

Transactions can be added here:

Add Transaction

Transaction Type
  • Money In — money going into the flat account
  • Money Out — money being spent
Payment Source
  • Flat Account/Card
  • Bank Transfer
  • Paid Personally

Dashboard

The dashboard shows the main things you will usually need:

  • Flat balance
  • Total money in
  • Total money out
  • Your pending claims
  • Your chore for the week
  • Recent transactions

Feel free to look through the other pages if you want more detail.

Using the Flat Card

The flat card is located on top of the fridge. If you use it, please record the transaction yourself and include a clear note in the description.

Transaction Type: Money Out
Payment Source: Flat Account/Card
Description: Include what it was for

Paying With Your Own Money

If you buy something for the flat using your own money, record it like this:

Transaction Type: Money Out
Payment Source: Paid Personally
Description: Include what it was for

The Paid By dropdown only needs to be completed when recording use of personal money or when sending money.
